Our two international branches — Montclair Turkiye and Montclair Thailand — serve as dedicated regional arms, each bringing local market depth to a globally connected network. Our structure was designed with one purpose in mind: to give investors a single, trusted partner capable of navigating multiple markets simultaneously. Whether a client is seeking a premium property in Dubai, a citizenship-by-investment asset in Türkiye, or a high-yield opportunity in the emerging markets of Southeast Asia, our group delivers end-to-end guidance from initial consultation through to post-acquisition support. Our Foundation What sets us apart is not merely the breadth of our geographic reach, but the depth of experience behind every decision we make. The team at the heart of Quick Talk Dubai and its Montclair branches has spent nearly a decade advising international investors across Istanbul, Dubai, and the wider region. That accumulated expertise — built through thousands of client engagements, cross-border transactions, and market cycles — remains fully intact and continues to drive our work today. Our leadership team has not changed. The professionals who built a reputation for integrity, precision, and results in the Istanbul and Dubai markets are the same people guiding our clients under the Quick Talk and Montclair banner. The Founder Fahed Mohamed Aladdin — Founder and CEO — brings more than 30 years of professional experience spanning construction, project management, and international real estate. His career began in the engineering and construction sector across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and the broader GCC region, where he held senior leadership roles overseeing major infrastructure, residential, hospitality, and institutional projects. Fahed's background as a Civil Engineer, combined with advanced studies in Business Administration, gave him a rare dual perspective: the technical rigour to evaluate a project's structural and financial viability, and the commercial acumen to identify where real value lies for an investor. Over the course of his career, he has directed project management offices, managed multi-billion-riyal construction programmes, and developed the cost-control, risk management, and contract administration frameworks that underpin sound real estate investment decisions. That is the lens through which we advise every client." — Fahed Mohamed Aladdin, Founder & CEO This construction-first mindset is a defining advantage of our advisory approach. Continuity of Expertise The core team that has guided investors across Istanbul and Dubai for nearly a decade continues to serve clients under our current structure, ensuring no loss of institutional knowledge or trusted relationships.
