# Surface: Public homepage

- **Mode:** Persuade (public home) with an Operate first action (search).
- **Audience:** Dubai buyers, renters, and investors arriving to find inventory.
- **Job:** First viewport starts a catalog search (sale / rent / project, location when available). Featured listings and brand story sit below, never instead of search.
- **Action:** Submit search → properties or projects listing with those params.
- **Proof:** Real published Convex records; empty states stay honest. No invented testimonials or stock-as-listing.
- **Direction:** Search-first catalog home inside locked BRAND.md (maroon, gold, Poppins/Inter, logo on light).
- **Memorable moment:** A full-viewport media field (owned QTRE plate or video at `public/brand/hero.mp4`); the catalog search is one white compound bar. Headline is the real tagline in maroon on the plate, no color wash over the video.
- **Composition:** White header + media hero + inventory below. Headline is the real tagline in Poppins — not a serif slogan, not stock-as-listing. No ticket. Video pauses when `prefers-reduced-motion` is set. Until `hero.mp4` exists, the field stays maroon.
- **Rejected:** Boarding-pass / airline ticket (perforations, stubs, stamps, airplanes, FROM/TO). QTRE is a brokerage, not a carrier. Header uses the real maroon lockup, not an invented travel mark.
- **Approved mock:** `.impeccable/mocks/home-comp-b2-type.png` — type-led search on maroon (BUY / RENT / OFF-PLAN + location bar).
- **Scope:** `app/[locale]/page.tsx` plus public nav/footer chrome this page requires.
- **Untouched:** Admin, portals, List Your Property funnel, commercial filter (no field yet).
- **Open:** Map provider (do not make the first viewport depend on a map).
